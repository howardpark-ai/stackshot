## description brief
"What is outstanding?" brief() shows the board at a glance: counts, the Machines panel, the receivers and the project
index (every open item: your move, on agents, waiting), then every open item in full as get shows it (its mover, its
open next steps, its `model:` line and, once a step is done or dropped, its `checklist:`), the items on hold after
them. For a doing item the board lists as waiting (see `model:` in get) the `model:` line ends with `· the board lists
it as waiting until <time> (<why>)`. A `state:` line shows only for an item that stores a state. An item's `project:`
line reads as in get. A line at the top says what changed in stackshot for 7 days after a release that changes a tool.
